How it ranks answers: it collects every answer it can find for the topic from Wikipedia (category pages and "List of…" pages) and a word database. It then ranks them by popularity, using Wikipedia page views over the last 30 days and how often each word is used in English. Low popularity means few other players will think of it. Answers with almost zero traffic are dropped, because the game may not accept something nobody has heard of.

Tip: if the results look off, edit the "Searching for" box to a simpler topic (e.g. "painters" instead of "a famous painter") or toggle the Wikipedia categories found.
